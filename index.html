01 /Engineering

Where materials meet software.

I study Industrial Engineering at Deggendorf Institute of Technology, European Campus Rottal-Inn. My interests span manufacturing, materials, simulation and engineering digitalisation.

Material database

Bachelor thesis · In progress

At Magnetfabrik Bonn, I am developing a material database for permanent magnet production. The project connects material characterisation with structured data management and a Python desktop application, helping make material information easier to access and use.

Manufacturing experience

Internship · Magnetfabrik Bonn

My internship covered toolmaking, production, research and development, and quality testing. Tasks included an Excel decision-support tool for injection moulding, material flow testing, mechanical testing and a plate-bending simulation in FreeCAD.

Technical direction

Areas I want to explore

I am particularly interested in simulation and electromagnetism: understanding physical behaviour and using computational tools to investigate engineering problems. Magnetic applications and audio technology bring several of my interests together.

02 /Music & creativity

A different way to create.

Rusiru Anuththara

Alongside engineering, I produce music and play guitar under the name Rusiru Anuththara. Music has been part of my life for around a decade, combining creative expression with an interest in how sound works.

Signal processing and audio technology connect this creative practice with my technical interests. I also enjoy visual design, working with Photoshop and Illustrator.
